# Confederate States Civilization Mod for UnCiv

A balanced UnCiv civilization mod centered on defensive warfare, plantation wealth, and Confederate naval pressure.

## Features

### Civilization Bonuses
- **Southern Defense**
  - +10% Strength when fighting in friendly territory
  - +15% Gold from Plantation tiles
  - -10% Loyalty from foreign civilizations

### Unique Units
- **Foot Cavalry** - agile melee unit with stronger early-pressure play
- **Stuart's Cavalry** - fast mounted raider and post-attack mover
- **Cherokee Rifles** - ranged pressure unit with anti-ranged utility
- **Blockade Runner** - naval raider and trade-support vessel
- **CSS Virginia** - heavy river and coastal melee warship
- **CSS Alabama** - commerce raider and anti-barbarian naval option
- **CSS Shenandoah** - fast ranged naval unit
- **Slave** - slower, weaker worker replacement that reflects Confederate labor and logistical limits
- **Conscript Militia** - militia support unit with construction utility
- **Confederate General** - elite leader unit with modest battlefield support

### Unique Buildings
- **Supply Depot** - improves production for gunpowder and melee/ranged units
- **Blockade Runner Port** - promotes gold and naval production
- **State Arsenal** - boosts military construction and unit strength

### Special Event
- **Rebellion** - a hard-rimmed civil disorder event for unhappy cities and strained loyalty

## Installation

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Copy the mod folder into your UnCiv mods directory.
3. Launch UnCiv and enable the mod from the in-game mod menu.

## Gameplay Balance

This iteration aims for a strong but fair Confederate identity:
- solid defensive play in your own territory
- plantation and trade economy
- modest military and naval pressure
- a labor handicap that keeps the civ distinctive without breaking the game
